# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **LoveLine** handles your information when you use our mobile application.

We care about privacy. LoveLine is designed as a personal and private space for couples to save memories, moments, emotions, and relationship timelines.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, LoveLine may collect or store the following types of information:

### Information You Provide

* Couple names
* Avatars
* Anniversary date
* Relationship memories
* Notes and text content
* Photos selected by you
* Timeline moments
* Quick emotions or emotional interactions
* App preferences such as theme, font, language, and background style

### Device and App Information

LoveLine may process limited technical information needed for app functionality, such as:

* Device type
* Operating system version
* App version
* Local settings
* Notification permission status
* Crash or diagnostic information, if enabled by the platform or future app tools

### Notification Information

If push notifications are enabled, LoveLine may process notification-related data such as:

* Device ID
* User ID
* Story ID
* Firebase token
* Platform
* Environment
* Active status
* Last seen time
* Created and updated timestamps

This information is used only to deliver app-related notifications.

## 2. Local Storage

LoveLine may store your app data locally on your device.

This can include couple information, memories, photos, emotional history, timeline data, widget data, and app preferences.

If your data is stored locally, it remains on your device unless you delete it, uninstall the app, reset your device, or remove related app data.

## 3. iCloud and CloudKit

LoveLine may support Apple iCloud or CloudKit features for sharing or syncing data between users or devices.

If you enable iCloud or CloudKit-related features, some of your data may be stored in or transmitted through Apple’s iCloud infrastructure.

Your use of iCloud is also subject to Apple’s own terms, privacy policy, and iCloud settings.

LoveLine does not control your Apple ID, iCloud account, iCloud storage, or Apple’s cloud infrastructure.

## 4. Photos, Camera, and Media

LoveLine may request access to your camera or photo library when you choose to:

* Add an avatar
* Add memory photos
* Add timeline photos
* Create or customize backgrounds
* Use photo-based widgets

LoveLine only accesses photos or camera features when you grant permission and use related features.

You can manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings.

## 5. Notifications

LoveLine may request notification permission to send reminders, couple updates, emotion alerts, memory reminders, or other app-related notifications.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

If Firebase Cloud Messaging or another notification provider is used, notification tokens may be processed only for the purpose of sending notifications.

## 6. Widgets

LoveLine may generate widget content from your saved app data, such as couple days, memories, images, or relationship moments.

Widget content may be visible on your device home screen. Please enable widgets only if you are comfortable with that information being visible on your device.

## 7. How We Use Information

LoveLine may use information to:

* Provide core app features
* Save and display couple memories
* Build your private relationship timeline
* Show widgets
* Personalize themes, fonts, and backgrounds
* Enable iCloud or CloudKit sharing, if supported
* Send notifications, if enabled
* Improve app stability and user experience
* Provide support when you contact us

## 8. Data Sharing

LoveLine does not sell your personal data.

LoveLine does not use your personal relationship content for advertising.

We may share or process limited data only when necessary for:

* App functionality
* iCloud or CloudKit features
* Push notification delivery
* App analytics or crash diagnostics, if introduced
* Legal compliance, if required by law

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

## 9. Third-Party Services

LoveLine may use third-party or platform services, including but not limited to:

* Apple iCloud
* Apple CloudKit
* Apple App Store services
* Firebase Cloud Messaging, if push notifications are enabled in future versions
* Crash reporting or analytics tools, if added in future versions

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## 10. Data Retention

LoveLine keeps your data for as long as needed to provide app features or until you delete it.

For locally stored data, deleting the app or app data may remove your information from the device.

For iCloud or CloudKit data, you may need to manage or delete data through the app, iCloud settings, or your Apple account settings.

Notification tokens may be updated or deactivated when you log out, change devices, disable notifications, or stop using related features.

## 11. Data Deletion

You may delete content inside the app where deletion features are available.

You may also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored data.

If iCloud sync or CloudKit sharing is enabled, deleting local app data may not always remove cloud-stored data immediately. You may need to manage iCloud data separately.

For support with deletion requests, contact us at the email below.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app data and reduce unauthorized access.

However, no method of storage, transmission, or synchronization is completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your device, Apple ID, passcode, and iCloud account secure.

## 13. Children’s Privacy

LoveLine is not specifically designed for children.

If you are under the age required by your country or region to use apps or provide consent, you should use LoveLine only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

## 14. International Users

LoveLine may be used in different countries or regions.

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide specific rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data.

You may contact us to request assistance regarding your personal data.

## 15. Your Choices

You can control your privacy by:

* Editing or deleting app content
* Disabling notifications
* Managing photo and camera permissions
* Managing iCloud settings
* Removing widgets from your home screen
* Deleting the app
* Contacting us for support
